Participation Framework 2026

Rules &
Guidelines.

Rules of engagement for all participants in the Constitutional Committee election process.

Introduction

Intersect is administering an election process for the Constitutional Committee (CC). This document outlines the the use of services provided by Intersect for this purpose. By taking part in this CC election process, participants indicate their acceptance of these guidelines.

Role of Intersect

Intersect's role is strictly focused on providing the platform and tools necessary for conducting this CC election. Intersect does not possess ownership of, nor exercise control over the Constitutional Committee. The CC operates as an independent entity.

Constitutional Framework

This election process is conducted based on the principles of the current Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Constitution. Participants involved in this election process are expected to be acquainted with and follow the principles and guidelines detailed in the Constitution.

Verified Credentials

To maintain the integrity of the committee election, all candidates must provide a Verified Cold Credential Hash. Participation is contingent upon Intersect's audit of these credentials to ensure correctness.

Scope of Responsibility

Intersect does not hold responsibility for any actions, decisions, or failures to act by the CC, its members, or any participants in this election process. Furthermore, Intersect is not accountable for the outcome of this election, the operational conduct of the CC, or the execution of any decisions made by the CC.

Eligibility & Information

The criteria determining eligibility to participate in a CC election process are established within the Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Constitution. Participants bear the responsibility for ensuring the accuracy and completeness of the information they provide during this election process. Intersect is not accountable for any inaccuracies or omissions in information supplied by participants.

Election Administration

This election process will follow the procedures and rules set forth by Intersect through approval by the Civics Committee and its peer elected members, ensuring alignment with the Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Constitution. Intersect retains the capacity to modify aspects of this election process should such changes be necessary to maintain a fair and transparent election.

Integrity & Compliance

If an application does not look genuine or appears incomplete, the CC election working group will attempt to contact the applicant via email for confirmation. If contact is unsuccessful, the working group reserves the right to hide the application from the public directory (applications will not be deleted). Decisions to hide profiles will be documented and communicated publicly.

In the event of a candidate not adhering to these guidelines, Intersect will flag the issue to the Civics Committee for their decision on the matter on a case-by-case basis. Any candidate who undergoes this review process will be contacted by Intersect to be informed about the ongoing evaluation and potential status changes.

Privacy & Disclosure

Through participation in this election process, applicants consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of their personal information as outlined in Intersect's privacy policy and Terms of Use. Unless indicated differently during the submission stage, any information provided by participants in relation to this election process might become publicly accessible.

Contact

Any questions or requests for clarification regarding these guidelines or this election process may be sent to constitution@intersectmbo.org.
