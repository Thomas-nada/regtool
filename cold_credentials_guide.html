Security Framework

Constitutional
Credentials.

Understanding the separation of power and security between Hot and Cold environments.

Cold Credentials

These are your "master" identity keys. They represent your permanent membership on the committee.

  • Stored Offline (Air-gapped)
  • Used for Authorizing Hot Keys
  • Rarely touched (High security)

Hot Credentials

These are your "operational" keys. They are used for day-to-day voting and governance actions.

  • Stored Online (Active Node)
  • Used for casting CC votes
  • Easily replaceable if compromised
Requirements

Cardano CLI

Latest version of cardano-cli installed on both Hot and Cold environments.

Synced Node

A fully synced Cardano node on the Hot environment to build and submit transactions.

Air-gapped PC

A dedicated machine that has never (and will never) be connected to the internet.

Physical Media

High-quality USB drives for "Sneakernet" data transfer between environments.

Best
Practices.

Safety standards for maintaining the integrity of the Cardano Constitution.

Never mix environments Never plug a Cold USB into an online machine. Use a dedicated bridge USB.
Backup physically Store Cold key backups (SKEY files) in physical safes in separate geographic locations.
Proactive Replacement Rotate Hot keys every 3–6 months even if no compromise is suspected.
