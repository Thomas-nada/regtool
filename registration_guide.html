DRep & Stake IDs

If you are a DRep or SPO, you can disclose your ID in your candidate profile

Candidate bio

A bio and motivation statement. Draft these in a document before pasting them into the form or write them directly.

Social media profile(s)

A link or handle to your social media profiles (X, LinkedIn, Telegram, Discord, GitHub or website).

Step 1: Choose Your Candidacy Track

Individual Roadmap

A streamlined process for subject-matter experts standing in a personal capacity.

1

Identity & Contact

Provide your full name, a valid email for verification, and select your primary region.

2

Governance Audit

Enter your DRep and Stake IDs to link your candidacy to your existing contributions.

3

Platform Statement

Submit your bio and detailed motivation. This content will be public in the Directory.

4

Final Verification

Paste your Cold Credential Hash to anchor your submission on the blockchain level.

Organisation Roadmap

For legal entities, development groups, and established community institutions.

1

Entity Details

Enter the official Organisation Name and designate a Lead Representative for coordination.

2

Institutional Platform

Describe the entity's mission and motivation for CC service, highlighting stability.

3

Transparency Plan

Explain how the organisation intends to keep its internal deliberations and votes transparent.

Consortium Roadmap

The comprehensive track for groups pooling diverse community expertise.

1

Consortium Identity

Establish the group name and lead contact point for the entire candidacy.

2

Roster Management

Add each member individually within the registration form to build the roster.

Post-Submission Protocol

Phase 1: Audit

The Election Authority audits your hash against on-chain data. Process typically takes 24-48 hours.

Phase 2: Visibility

Once verified, your profile will automatically appear in the public Candidate Directory.
